Point of view

A working prototype is not the summit. It is base camp.

Fig. 01 — Define the mine problem

Many technologies look promising in a laboratory, a pilot, or an adjacent industry. Mining adoption depends on more: the technology has to solve a real operational problem, fit the mine workflow, be demonstrated credibly in the field, and then mature into a product that can survive, be validated, manufactured, installed, serviced, supported and scaled.

Mining technology teams get stuck on what we call Approval Mountain. Each customer requirement, site constraint, field observation, certification need, manufacturing limitation, service requirement or distributor expectation reveals another readiness gate. The fastest way to build the wrong mining product is to start with the technology instead of the mine problem, the operating context, and the proof required.

Approval Mountain is a communication framework, not a claim of control over third-party approvals.

Readiness domains

Mine-readiness is assessed across seven domains.

A demonstrator can prove valuable technical capability and still be years away from repeatable deployment. These are the domains examined in a Mine-Ready Productization Assessment.

  • Product and system architecture
  • Mine environment and ruggedization
  • Embedded, application, cloud, data and communications readiness
  • Verification, validation, certification and compliance path
  • Manufacturability, testability and supply-chain readiness
  • Deployment, service and lifecycle support
  • Channel and execution readiness

Delivery model

One technical lead. The right delivery capacity.

Fig. 02 — Delivery model

Spyke Technologies provides senior mining-technology product leadership where it creates the most value: mine-operational translation, requirements and interface definition, productization strategy, technical integration, readiness-gate leadership, risk reduction, partner coordination and capability transfer.

Detailed implementation work is performed by your team or by qualified specialists where appropriate. Client-direct contracting with engineering firms, laboratories, manufacturers, installers, service providers and channel partners is the default — so the engagement does not become the critical path, and you are left more capable rather than more dependent.

Working boundaries

Clear responsibilities.

You retain product ownership, commercial decisions, customer relationships, expenditures, direct supplier and partner relationships wherever practical, final product-release authority, and manufacturer-of-record responsibility.

Spyke Technologies is not generic strategy consulting, a staffing agency, a contract manufacturer, a system integrator of record, a certification laboratory or body, a sales-representation service, or a substitute for your manufacturer-of-record responsibilities. No engagement guarantees mine-site access, customer approval, pilot sponsorship, certification, market fit or commercial success.

Spyke Technologies provides independent consulting based on general professional experience, independent analysis and publicly available information. Confidential, proprietary or trade-secret information belonging to any current or former employer, client, supplier or partner is not used, disclosed or made accessible.
