About

Senior mining-technology judgment, applied at the stage where it matters.

Spyke Technologies Inc. is an independent consultancy based in the Montréal region of Quebec, working with Canadian and international technology teams entering or scaling in mining.

Sandy Pyke

President — Spyke Technologies Inc.

Principal

More than 30 years in mining technology, spanning product and system development, field deployment, global support, manufacturing readiness, and dealer and channel enablement.

Career built in mining equipment and industrial technology: electronics and embedded systems, industrial sensing, hardware deployment in harsh environments, and the technical leadership of engineering organizations delivering products into production and the field.

The recurring pattern across those years is the same one this practice is built around: capable technology arriving at a mine site without the application definition, the demonstrator evidence, or the productization work required to become something a mine can buy, deploy, service and support.

Work is conducted in English and French, with a Quebec and Canadian operating context.

Background

Practice areas.

  • Mining equipment and industrial technology systems
  • Industrial sensing, electronics and embedded hardware
  • Product and system architecture, requirements and interface control
  • Ruggedization, validation and certification-path planning
  • Manufacturing readiness, testability and serviceability
  • Field deployment, service, support and channel enablement
  • Technical leadership, governance and capability transfer

Engagement principles

How the work is run.

Decision-focused

Every engagement produces a defined next decision, not a document that describes the situation you already knew you were in.

Evidence before opinion

Findings are grounded in your architecture, test results, field observations and deployment context — and corrected when you correct them.

Capability transfer by design

A successful engagement leaves your team more capable and less dependent. Making Spyke Technologies the critical path is a failure mode, not a business model.

Working boundaries

What this practice is, and is not.

You retain product ownership, commercial decisions, customer relationships, expenditures, direct supplier and partner relationships wherever practical, final product-release authority, and manufacturer-of-record responsibility.

Spyke Technologies is not generic strategy consulting, a staffing agency, a contract manufacturer, a system integrator of record, a certification laboratory or body, a sales-representation service, or a substitute for your manufacturer-of-record responsibilities. No engagement guarantees mine-site access, customer approval, pilot sponsorship, certification, market fit or commercial success.

Spyke Technologies provides independent consulting based on general professional experience, independent analysis and publicly available information. Confidential, proprietary or trade-secret information belonging to any current or former employer, client, supplier or partner is not used, disclosed or made accessible.
